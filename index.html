Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What building services does Essex Improvements provide?

We provide a comprehensive range of building services, including house extensions, full home renovations, roofing, kitchens and bathrooms, landscaping, drainage and sewer repairs, septic tank systems, double glazing, damp proofing, timber treatment, and disabled adaptations.

How long has Essex Improvements been established?

We began trading as a family-run builder in 1990 and incorporated as Essex Improvements Ltd in 2003. We have over 30 years of continuous experience in the local Essex building trade.

Do you build house extensions in Essex?

Yes, house extensions are one of our core specialties. We handle everything from single-storey kitchen additions to complex double-storey home extensions, managing the entire project from groundworks to the final internal finish.

Are your tradesmen fully qualified and certified?

Yes. All our electrical work is completed by certified NICEIC operatives, and all plumbing and heating works are carried out by Gas Safe registered professionals. We are also proud to be affiliated with the Federation of Master Builders (FMB).

Do you work across all of Essex?

We take on projects throughout Essex. Get in touch with your location and we'll confirm we can cover it.

How do I get a quote?

Call us or send an enquiry with a brief summary of the work. We'll arrange a site visit and follow up with a written quote.

Planning a building project in Essex?

Tell us what you're looking to build, renovate or improve, and we'll get back to you with next steps.
